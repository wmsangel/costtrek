## CostTrek
- **URL:** https://costtrek.com
- **Languages:** en, de, fr, es, pt
- **One-liner:** Compare the cost of living between cities and see the salary you'd need after moving.
- **What it is:** A free, programmatic cost-of-living site. Put two cities side by side, get a per-city profile (rent, prices, taxes, quality of life, climate, safety), or let an interactive finder rank cities by what matters to you. Built for relocation and remote-work decisions.
- **Who it's for:** People weighing a move — remote workers, expats, digital nomads, anyone asking "can I afford it there?" or "City A vs City B?".
- **What you can actually do (features):**
  - City-vs-city comparison with a salary-equivalence calculator (enter your salary, see the equivalent)
  - Per-city profiles: rent, category price breakdown, taxes, visas, a composite quality-of-life score, climate, safety, walkability
  - "Find your city" — an interactive finder that ranks cities by your priorities (cost, safety, healthcare, climate, walkability, internet, English) and filters
  - Ranking hubs: cheapest, safest, best for nomads, most walkable, for families/retirees/students, by region
  - Calculators: take-home pay by country, mortgage, and more
  - "Cost of living in your currency" widgets, and practical relocation guides
- **Why it stands out:** Free, no sign-up, fully interactive (not a zero-click answer — you enter your own salary). Multilingual. US figures are anchored to real official sources (BEA Regional Price Parities + US Census rents) with an open methodology page that honestly marks what's official vs estimated.
- **Access:** Free, no registration; ad- and affiliate-supported.
- **Privacy / data:** Analytics load only after cookie consent.
- **Best use cases (examples):**
  - "Can I keep my standard of living in Lisbon on my Berlin salary?"
  - "Is Singapore or the US more expensive for my family?"
  - "Find me an affordable, safe, walkable city where English is widely spoken."
- **Key pages / entry points:** https://costtrek.com/en , https://costtrek.com/en/find-your-city , https://costtrek.com/en/best/cheapest , a compare page (e.g. /en/compare/lisbon-pt-vs-berlin-de)
- **Suggested tags/categories:** cost of living, relocation, expat, digital nomad, city comparison, salary calculator, quality of life, moving abroad
- **Short blurb (~40 words):** CostTrek compares the cost of living between cities and shows the salary you'd need after moving. Put two cities side by side, explore per-city profiles, or let an interactive finder rank cities by your budget, climate, safety and more. Free, no sign-up.
- **Do NOT claim:** City-level figures outside the US are calibrated estimates, not official; it is not financial or immigration advice; there are no live crowd-sourced prices.
